# Account and Data Deletion - FootFall by GK

At FootFall by GK, we value your privacy and give you full control over your data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you can do so easily.

## How to delete your account

### Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)
The fastest way to delete your data is directly within the app:
1. Open the **FootFall by GK** app.
2. Navigate to the **Profile** tab.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **🗑️ Delete Account**.
4. Confirm your action. This will immediately and permanently delete:
   - Your Firebase Authentication account.
   - Your step history, hydration, and nutrition profile from our cloud database.
   - Your global leaderboard entries.

### Option 2: Request Deletion via Email / Web
If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can request manual deletion:
1. Send an email to **ravitejafine@gmail.com** from the email address associated with your FootFall account.
2. Use the subject line: **"Account Deletion Request - FootFall by GK"**.
3. You can also visit our online deletion page at [https://ravitejakummara.github.io/FootFall_Privacy_Policy/account-deletion.html](https://ravitejakummara.github.io/FootFall_Privacy_Policy/account-deletion.html).
4. We will process your request and wipe all your data from our servers within 7 business days.

## Data that will be deleted:
- Your Name and Email address.
- Your entire Step & Walking History.
- Your Hydration & Food Intake (Meal) Logs.
- Your Age, Weight, and Height profile.
- All leaderboard records.

**Note:** Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
